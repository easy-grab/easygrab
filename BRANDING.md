# Easy Grab Branding

Official name: **Easy Grab**  
Ticker: **GRAB**

## Core Line

> Grab SOL in one click.

## Product Positioning

Easy Grab turns SOL into funded claim links for QR drops, event rewards, private payouts, and launch campaigns.

Recipients can claim in two ways:

- Connect a wallet and receive the full amount.
- Paste a Solana address and receive without connecting a wallet.

## Economic Model

- Minimum claim: 0.01 SOL
- Platform fee: 1% of funded rewards, minimum 0.001 SOL
- Fee use: marketing and ecosystem growth

## Tone

- Fast
- Direct
- Dark-native
- Useful before it explains itself
